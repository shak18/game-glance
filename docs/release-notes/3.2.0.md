# Game Glance 3.2.0

A Game Glance screensaver. Update from Quick Access → Game Glance → Updates.

- **Your games in Steam's screensaver.** Game Glance now draws Steam's Game Slideshow (Settings → Customization →
  Screensaver) with the games of your Games row, your recent games or the chosen collection, instead of Steam's recent
  list. Each game's art fills the screen, your custom SteamGridDB art first, with Steam's 20 seconds per game, crossfade
  and slow drift.
- **Logo, stats and status bar.** The game's logo at the bottom left, as on Home. A small card at the bottom right with
  your play time, the HowLongToBeat time you are working toward and your achievements. Home's status bar at the top
  right. Each is a switch in Quick Access → Game Glance → Screensaver, all on by default; turn all three off for only
  the art. **Preview** shows it at once.
- **Any screen.** The logo, the card and the status bar fit and stay readable on 16:9, 16:10, ultrawide, 4:3 and square
  screens, and on the handheld.
- **Nothing to look up.** The times and achievement counts are what Game Glance already saved on the device.
- **Needs a Steam client with the screensaver.** On an older one, Quick Access says so and Steam's screensaver stays as
  it is. Steam's Bouncing Logo, or the switch off, gives you Steam's own screensaver.
- **Updates and Home.** After Game Glance updates while Home is open, Home is opened again so it shows the new version:
  before, its status bar could lose the battery and connection until you left Home.
