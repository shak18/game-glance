# Game Glance 3.0.6

Status bar items. Coming from 3.0.x, update from Quick Access → Game Glance → Updates.

- **Battery.** The battery is green while the device is charging.
- **Downloads.** The status bar shows a download and its percent while Steam downloads.
- **Your avatar.** Turn on "Profile picture in the status bar" in Quick Access (Spotlight Home) to show your avatar, round,
  with a ring in your online colour, in place of the dot. It's off by default.
- **Quick Access.** The status bar stays on screen while Quick Access is open.
- **Game page.** Steam's top bar no longer flickers behind the status bar as a game page opens.
